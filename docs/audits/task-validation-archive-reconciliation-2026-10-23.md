## Additional contradiction gap

The polarity collector records positive and negative flags in a bitmask. The
comparison rejected a purely opposite flag, but a mixed positive-and-negative
mask was equal to neither individual flag and could pass. Three new examples
failed before repair: positive requirement with contradictory evidence,
negative requirement with contradictory evidence, and a contradictory requirement
with one-sided evidence.

Compare the opposite bits rather than requiring exact mask equality. A shared
concept with conflicting polarity cannot satisfy the criterion by concept
overlap alone. Positive controls retain consistent positive and negative claims
and an unrelated negated concept. A full-plan test rejects mixed delivery
statements without assigning criterion evidence.
